Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Pictured is an app for iPhone and Apple TV that shows your photos as framed pictures on your TV. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we do not collect any of it.

What we collect

Nothing. Pictured has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send any information about you or your photos to us or to anyone else.

Your photos

When you choose a photo, Pictured asks for permission to see your photo library so you can pick from it. The framed pictures you make are stored on your own devices and in your own private iCloud storage, so that your iPhone and Apple TV show the same thing. That storage belongs to your Apple Account. We cannot see or access it.

If you delete a picture in the app, it is removed from your devices and from your iCloud storage. You can remove everything by deleting the app’s data under iCloud in your device’s Settings.

Your home network

Pictured may ask for permission to find devices on your local network. This is used only so your iPhone can tell your own Apple TV about a change right away. Nothing is sent outside your home network this way, and the app works without it.

Purchases

The optional Pictured Unlimited purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple’s handling of your information is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

Pictured does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Pictured support page.
