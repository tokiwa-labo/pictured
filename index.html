Pictured Support

Pictured shows your photos as framed pictures on your Apple TV. Here are answers to common questions.

My Apple TV isn’t showing what I made on my iPhone

Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Changes travel through iCloud, so allow a little time after making one, and check that both devices are connected to the internet.

Changes take a while to appear on the TV

Pictured can update your TV instantly when your iPhone and Apple TV are on the same Wi-Fi. For that, the iPhone app needs Local Network access: open Settings on your iPhone, go to Apps, choose Pictured, and turn on Local Network.

Can I use Pictured without an iPhone?

Yes. On the Apple TV, press the centre button on the remote while a picture is showing to open the menu. From there you can add pictures, make galleries and change settings.

How do I pause on one picture?

On the Apple TV, press Play/Pause on the remote. On the iPhone, use the pause button in the bar at the bottom of the screen, or open a picture and choose Show on TV.

What does the free version include?

One gallery with up to 10 pictures, the single-photo and two-photo layouts, and the Walnut and Black frames. Pictured Unlimited is a one-time purchase that adds every layout and frame and removes the limits. Other layouts and frames can be previewed before buying.

I bought Pictured Unlimited but it isn’t unlocked

Open Settings in the app and choose Restore Purchase. Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase, or to an account in the same Family Sharing group.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
